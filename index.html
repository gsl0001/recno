01 / The photos you already took

Sort jobsite photos from your camera roll by job.

Your camera roll is the jobsite record. It just isn't sorted. Sifter matches the photos taken near a saved job and stacks them for you to approve. Nothing is filed without you saying yes.

06 / Where your work lives

Where does Recno store my jobsite photos?

On your phone, not on our server. Recno has no accounts and no sync service, because there is nothing on our side to sync to. Here is what leaves the device, and when.

Your records

Photos, notes, hours and reports are stored on the device. We do not receive them and cannot read them.

Backups

With automatic backup on, archives go to Recno's own private iCloud container — between you and Apple, not through us.

Weather stamps

On out of the box: coordinates go to Open-Meteo to fetch the conditions for a shot. Switch weather off in Settings and nothing is sent.

Addresses and places

Searching a job address sends what you type, and roughly where you are, to OpenStreetMap. Naming a job from your location asks Apple for the street address.

Subscriptions

The subscription SDK checks your entitlement with RevenueCat when the app starts. It never receives jobsite content.

Counting installs

An anonymous activation signal you can opt into, off unless you turn it on. No profile, no tracking, no ads anywhere in the app.

Full detail in the privacy policy and delete data pages.

08 / Before you download

Questions contractors ask first.

Do I or my crew need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You clock the crew in and out from your own phone, so they don't need the app installed.

Does it work without cell signal?

Yes. Capture, filing, the daily log and reports all work offline because the records live on the phone. Weather stamps and address search are the parts that need a connection.

What happens to my photos if I lose or replace my phone?

Automatic backup is on by default and saves to Recno's private iCloud container under your Apple Account. You can also export a backup file yourself before switching phones. See Support for the details.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Recno is iPhone only.

What do I get free, and can I cancel Pro?

Capture, filing, the daily log, the crew time clock, punch lists and backup are free. Pro adds Sifter, report export and timesheet export. It is an App Store subscription, so you cancel it in your Apple Account settings like any other.
