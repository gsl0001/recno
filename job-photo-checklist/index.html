BEFORE / DURING / AFTER

A small repair.
A clear photo record.

For handymen and small repair contractors: take photos that explain what you found, what you changed and what still needs attention. Use this checklist for a simple client update.

1. Before: show the job and the issue

  • Take a wide shot so the client can identify the room, fixture or cabinet.
  • Take a close-up of the issue. Add a short caption naming the location and what is visible.
  • Record existing damage or access limits without guessing their cause.

2. During: capture what will be hidden

  • Photograph relevant work before it is covered, where safe and permitted.
  • Write what changed in plain language. Separate an observation from your diagnosis.
  • For unfinished items, name the next action, responsible person and agreed due date if known.

3. After: make the result easy to compare

  • Take the finished wide shot from roughly the same position as the first photo.
  • Add a detail shot of the repair, and a caption explaining what was done.
  • State remaining items honestly. A photograph alone does not prove a repair is safe, compliant or accepted.

Example: cabinet-door repair

Before: wide cabinet view and a hinge detail. During: the visible fastening change. After: matching cabinet view, hinge detail and any remaining adjustment.
